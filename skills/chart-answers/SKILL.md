---
name: chart-answers
description: How to answer when the user asks for a chart, graph, plot, visual or dashboard.
keywords: chart, graph, plot, visual, visualise, visualize, dashboard, pie, bar chart, line chart, trend, infographic
---
- The app draws the chart itself, straight from the file's cells, and shows it under your answer. Do NOT write chart JSON, ASCII bars or image links, and do not make up values for a chart.
- Write 2-4 short lines: what the chart compares, the largest and the smallest item with their values, and one notable point (for example, one item making up most of the total).
- Cite the sheet or page each value comes from, as usual.
- If the user asks for a dashboard, list the 3-5 most important totals with citations. The dashboard link is added automatically.
