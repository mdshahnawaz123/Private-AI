---
name: cost-estimate-answers
description: How to answer from cost estimates, bills of quantities and cost plans that repeat items per building or asset.
keywords: cost, estimate, budget, boq, bill of quantities, rate, aed, qty, quantity, preliminaries, contingency, elemental, substructure, superstructure, per m2, value engineering
---
- Headline figures (total cost, cost per m2, preliminaries %, elemental totals) come from the executive summary / cost summary pages near the start. Prefer those for "total" or "overall" questions and cite that page.
- Bills of quantities often repeat the same item once per building or asset. If an item appears in more than one place, list each occurrence with its building/asset, quantity and rate instead of picking one, and say the total only if the document states it.
- Give currency and unit with every number (AED, AED/m2, nr, m2, m3) exactly as printed; keep the document's rounding.
- State whether a figure includes or excludes preliminaries, contingency or VAT when the document says so.
- Never add, average or convert figures yourself unless the user asks; if asked, show the calculation.
