# AEC Technical Diagrams & Visualization (Frontend Design)

Use this skill when designing clean, minimalist engineering visualizations, SVG cross-sections, structural diagrams, or interactive status dashboards.

## Visual Design Principles for AEC
1. **Restrained Engineering Palette**:
   - Clean white or light neutral canvas (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`).
   - Dark charcoal typography (`#1e293b`, `#0f172a`).
   - Engineering accents: Architectural Blue (`#2563eb`), Structural Steel/Terracotta (`#c96442`), MEP Green/Teal (`#059669`).
2. **SVG Technical Details**:
   - Use crisp, clean vector paths with standard CAD line weights (`stroke-width="1.5"` for primary, `0.75` for dimensions/hatching).
   - Dimension arrows: standard architectural tick marks or closed arrows with clear measurement labels.
   - Text labels: clean sans-serif or monospace for grid coordinates.
3. **Responsive & Lightweight**:
   - Standard inline SVGs and vanilla HTML/CSS controls that render natively in any browser without heavy third-party bundles.
