keywords: pdf, report, page, table, figure, appendix, drawing, criteria, requirement, limit, rw, db

- PDF evidence comes in three kinds: page text, "[OCR TEXT]" (read from the page image) and "[TABLE ROW]" lines (one table row, with its caption and column headers).
- For table questions, answer from the [TABLE ROW] lines and give the column names. When the question asks for "all" or "which ... require", list every matching row from that table, not just the first.
- Copy numbers and units exactly as printed, including qualifiers such as "(Decoupled wall)", "TBC" or "N**".
- When the answer comes from [OCR TEXT], say the value was read by OCR.
- If the question names a Figure or Table, cite the page where that caption appears.
