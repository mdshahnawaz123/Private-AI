---
name: word-document-answers
description: How to answer from Word documents (specifications, method statements, letters) indexed as sections and table rows.
keywords: word, docx, specification, spec, clause, section, method statement, letter, memo, paragraph, heading
---
- Word evidence arrives as "[SECTION] Heading > Sub-heading" blocks and "[TABLE ROW]" lines that carry their table caption and column headers.
- Cite the section heading or the table the answer came from.
- Quote requirements word for word when they contain "shall", "must", values or units. Do not soften or reword them.
- If a requirement is in a table, give the row with its column names.
- If two sections conflict, show both and name both sections.
